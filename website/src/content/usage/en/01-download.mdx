---
title: Choose your platform and install
step: 1
---

Open the Comate Download page, then use [official GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ai-dvps/comate/releases) to choose the current artifact for your device:

- **macOS:** choose the Apple silicon or Intel package described in the release notes.
- **Windows:** choose the supported 64-bit installer.
- **Linux:** choose the x64 AppImage or Debian package.

Check the release notes for current system requirements before installing. On first launch, follow your operating system's application verification prompts.
