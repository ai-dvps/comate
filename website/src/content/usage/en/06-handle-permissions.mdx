---
title: Handle permission and attention requests
step: 6
---

When an action crosses a permission boundary, review the requested tool and scope before choosing **Allow**, **Allow always**, or **Deny**. Use the narrowest approval that fits the task.

**If the task needs permission or attention:** the Session pauses instead of silently bypassing the boundary. Return to the permission card, structured question, or embedded browser takeover; approve, deny, or provide the missing input. The Agent continues only after that decision.
