---
question: How does Comate connect to internal models, IM, and a Skill Market?
order: 4
---

An organization can configure internal model Providers, approved IM entry points, and an internal Skill Market. Within those boundaries, Comate can receive tasks, use approved capabilities, and return status plus report links to the work context. Availability depends on identity, network, permission, and service configuration supplied by the organization; these integrations are not presented as enabled by default.
